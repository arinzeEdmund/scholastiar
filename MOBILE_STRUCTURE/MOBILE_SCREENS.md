## Opportunity Detail Template

All opportunity detail screens should share a template:

- title
- provider/organization
- destination country
- mobility/funding/visa tags
- deadline
- application time
- success score
- readiness percentage
- eligibility
- documents needed
- application steps
- source/provider trust
- save/apply/board actions

## Mobile Article Reader

Article/news/immigration update screens should show:

- title
- last updated date
- short answer
- source/disclaimer where needed
- content
- related opportunities
- readiness CTA
- newsletter/alert subscription CTA
