## Recommended Root Tabs

Use five bottom tabs:

```txt
Discover
Saved
Apply
Alerts
Me
```

Why:

- `Discover` supports search and opportunity browsing
- `Saved` supports comparison and future action
- `Apply` centralizes tracker, AI Apply Agent, and Apply For Me
- `Alerts` handles deadlines, digests, and readiness nudges
- `Me` handles profile, documents, subscription, and settings
