# Seed Data

Status: Development data guide

## Purpose

Seed data should make the app testable and visually realistic without using real personal data.

## Seed Categories

### Countries And Cities

Include common target countries:

- United Kingdom
- United States
- Canada
- Germany
- Australia
- Ireland
- Netherlands
- United Arab Emirates

### Employers

Create sample employers:

- verified university hiring its own students (campus jobs)
- retail or hospitality employer hiring students part-time
- technology company hiring post-study graduates
- healthcare employer with graduate roles
- employer whose hours exceed typical study visa limits, for contrast

### Jobs

Create:

- part-time term-time student job
- holiday-only student job
- campus job (library or student ambassador)
- internship or placement
- post-study graduate role with employer-confirmed work visa sponsorship, startable on a post-study permit
- post-study graduate role sponsored from day one
- student job whose hours exceed typical study visa limits (for contrast)

### Candidates

Create sample candidates with:

- complete profile
- incomplete profile
- study visa work conditions or a post-study permit
- different skill sets

Do not use real identities.

### Applications

Create sample statuses:

- draft
- submitted
- viewed
- shortlisted
- interview
- rejected

### Messages

Create application-linked candidate/employer threads.

### Relocation (Russia first)

Create, all marked **unverified demo data** (never activated in production):

- Russia as a destination with base Pre-Arrival and Post-Arrival steps, emergency numbers and cost lines
- two embassy layers to show the difference: the Russian embassy for applicants resident in **Nigeria** and in **Rwanda** (different documents, fees in local currency, appointment process)
- visa types: student, preparatory language course
- Moscow as a city layer (offices, maps, transport notes, deadlines, living costs) and one university layer
- accommodation: a university dormitory and a verified private provider with rooms and availability
- communities: approved and pending examples across several faiths and groups
- pilots: one Verified freelancer and one Staff pilot, with service packages and cohort prices
- pilot incentives: a referral code per pilot; commissions pending (in hold), payable, paid and clawed back; one award cycle in voting with votes, and last year's published winners (global + Russia)
- a cohort forming for one arrival window
- journeys: one student mid-way through Pre-Arrival, one in Post-Arrival, one at 100% (Handsoff eligible), one Year Check-in member with an open support case
- payout tasks: due, paid (with proof) and overdue

## Rule

Seed data should help test UI states: empty, partial, active, error, and complete.
