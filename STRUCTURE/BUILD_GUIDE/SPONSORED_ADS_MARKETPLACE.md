# Sponsored Ads And Sponsorship Marketplace

Status: Advertising, sponsored placement, and B2B monetization source of truth

## Purpose

The Sponsored Ads And Sponsorship Marketplace lets verified employers, universities, opportunity providers, migration agencies, training providers, and approved partners pay for visibility on Scholastiar.ai.

This system should create B2B revenue without damaging user trust.

It should support:

- sponsored job posts
- promoted visa-sponsored jobs
- featured employer profiles
- sponsored university programs
- sponsored scholarship/fellowship/grant listings
- featured migration agency profiles
- sponsored training programs
- sponsored conference, competition, and award listings
- newsletter sponsorships
- sponsored articles/guides
- country page sponsorships
- dashboard recommendation placements
- hiring/recruitment campaign packages
- provider campaign packages
- migration consultation campaigns

Core principle:

```txt
Sponsored visibility is allowed.
Hidden influence is not allowed.
```

Every sponsored item must be clearly labeled, moderated, and useful to the user.

## Trust First Rule

Scholastiar.ai users may be making serious career, education, immigration, and financial decisions.

Sponsored content must never override:

- safety
- legitimacy
- eligibility accuracy
- opportunity quality
- user privacy
- moderation
- clear labeling
- platform trust

Sponsored content should help users discover relevant opportunities faster. It should not trick users, create false hope, or make weak opportunities look stronger than they are.

- international student recruitment campaigns

### Opportunity Providers

Scholarship, grant, fellowship, competition, conference, training, and award providers can sponsor:

Migration agencies can sponsor:

- verified agency profiles

Purpose:

- place sponsor messages inside relevant email digests

Examples:

- sponsored job pack in weekly jobs digest
- sponsored scholarship provider in scholarship digest

- sponsored university in study abroad newsletter

Rule:

```txt
Newsletter sponsorships must be clearly labeled and relevant to the digest topic.
```

### Sponsored Article Or Guide

Purpose:

- publish sponsor-supported educational content through the Publishing Intelligence Engine

Examples:

- "How nurses can prepare for UK care sponsorship roles"
- "What international students should know before applying to German universities"
- "Documents to prepare before booking a Canada relocation consultation"

Rules:

- article must be useful
- sponsorship must be disclosed
- claims must be checked
- official/credible sources should be linked where needed
- sponsor cannot buy false claims

### Country Page Sponsorship

Purpose:

- let verified partners sponsor relevant country pages

Examples:

- Canada jobs page
- Germany scholarships page
- UK care jobs page
- Australia study page

Rule:

```txt
Sponsor placement must not make it look like Scholastiar.ai officially endorses success, admission, migration, or visa approval.
```

### Dashboard Recommendation Placement

Purpose:

Guardrail:

- targeting must be privacy-safe

```txt
AI Apply Agent and Apply For Me areas may show sponsored opportunities only if they are clearly labeled and still match the user's stated goals and eligibility.
```

## Targeting Model

Sponsors may target broad segments.

Possible targeting dimensions:

- user country of residence
- desired destination country
- profession
- industry
- education level
- opportunity type
- language preference
- plan type
- saved opportunity category
- readiness score band
- document readiness band
- deadline urgency
- relocation interest
- university interest
- scholarship interest

Privacy rule:

```txt
Sponsors must not receive private user data or individual readiness scores unless a user explicitly applies, submits an inquiry, books a service, or gives consent.
```

Use aggregated campaign reporting by default.

## Labeling Standards

Use clear labels:

```txt
Sponsored
Promoted Opportunity
Promoted Job
Featured Employer
Featured University
Featured Provider
Featured Migration Agency
Sponsored Guide
Newsletter Sponsor
```

Do not use vague labels that hide payment.

Avoid:

```txt
Recommended by Scholastiar.ai
Best guaranteed option
Approved migration path
Guaranteed visa
Guaranteed job
Guaranteed admission
```

## Moderation Rules

All sponsors must pass verification before paid promotion.

Moderation checks:

- organization identity
- website/domain
- contact details
- opportunity legitimacy
- role/program/service accuracy
- immigration claim safety
- payment/refund clarity where applicable
- user complaint history
- prohibited claims
- scam/fraud signals

Admin must be able to:

- approve sponsor
- reject sponsor
- pause campaign
- remove sponsored placement
- refund or void a campaign where needed
- ban sponsor
- view complaints
- review campaign analytics

## Prohibited Content

Do not allow ads that claim:

- guaranteed visa approval
- guaranteed job placement without proof and legal basis
- guaranteed scholarship/admission
- fake government affiliation
- fake embassy connection
- pay-to-get-visa shortcuts
- misleading proof of funds services
- document fraud
- fake credentials
- unrealistic migration promises
- hidden fees
- exploitative recruitment

Featured migration agency:       $99-$999/month

Country page sponsorship:        $299-$2,500/month
Hiring campaign package:         $499-$5,000+

Pricing depends on:

- audience size

- category demand

## Relationship To Other Systems

### Pricing

Sponsored products should be represented in pricing and billing as add-ons, campaign purchases, or recurring sponsor plans.

### Publishing Intelligence Engine

Sponsored articles, guides, newsletters, and content placements should follow the Publishing Intelligence Engine's humanization, accuracy, and disclosure rules.

### Email Intelligence

Sponsored email placements should only appear in relevant digests and must be clearly labeled.

### Growth Strategy

Sponsored placements create B2B revenue and help Scholastiar.ai move toward $1m MRR without depending only on applicant subscriptions.

### Security And RLS

Sponsors should only access their own campaigns, billing, and aggregated performance data.

Private user profile, document, readiness score, and application data must remain protected unless the user directly consents through an application, inquiry, or booking flow.
