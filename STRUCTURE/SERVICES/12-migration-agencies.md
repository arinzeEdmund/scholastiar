# Migration Agencies

Status: Unified Platform Service

## Feature Vision

Scholastiar Migration Agencies is a trusted office and agency service layer where users can find Scholastiar-owned offices and verified partner agencies, view services and prices, book appointments, submit inquiries, upload documents for pre-check, communicate with consultants, and receive real-world support for study, work, visa, travel, and relocation processes.

This is not the core AI platform. It is a trust and offline-support layer for users who still want to speak to real people, visit a real office, or work with traditional travel consultants while pursuing cross-border study, work, travel, relocation, or migration opportunities.

Suggested ownership model:

- 95% Scholastiar physical offices in major cities worldwide
- 5% verified third-party partner agencies

The core idea:

> Users can discover trusted migration/travel support offices, view their services and prices, book appointments, submit inquiries, and receive responses inside Scholastiar.ai.

Every agency or office service should support the broader greener-pasture mission: helping users move from their current country toward a country of choice through clear, ethical, document-ready, and expectation-managed guidance.

The emotional promise:

> If you want real human guidance, there is a trusted office you can visit.

## Problem Being Solved

Some users will not fully trust AI-only or online-only processes.

They may want:

- to visit an office
- to speak to a real consultant
- to ask questions face-to-face
- to confirm documents physically
- to involve parents, sponsors, or family members
- to receive reassurance before paying or applying
- to work with a consultant in their own city or language
- to get help with complex travel, study, work, visa, or relocation processes

This feature gives Scholastiar.ai a physical trust layer and keeps support inside a controlled, transparent platform instead of scattered WhatsApp chats and informal agents.

## AI Opportunities

### Service Recommendation AI

AI can recommend the right service based on a user's situation.

Example:

> You are applying to Russian universities and have an admission offer. You likely need document review, translation/legalization guidance, and student visa checklist support.

### Inquiry Triage AI

AI can summarize user inquiries for consultants and suggest:

- service category
- likely documents needed
- urgency level
- country-specific notes
- suggested appointment type

### Document Pre-Check AI

AI can assist consultants by identifying:

- missing documents
- expired documents
- documents needing translation
- documents needing legalization
- mismatched names or dates
- unclear scans

AI should support consultants, not replace regulated legal judgment.

### Follow-Up Summary AI

After appointments, AI can help generate:

- consultation summary
- next-step checklist
- document checklist
- follow-up message
- appointment report
