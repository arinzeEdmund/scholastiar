# Apply For Me

Status: Unified Platform Service

## Feature Vision

Scholastiar Apply For Me is a specialized human application marketplace where customers hire vetted human operators, called Scholastiar Forwarders, to apply to opportunities on their behalf.

It is similar to an Upwork-style marketplace, but purpose-built for applications across:

- jobs
- scholarships
- universities
- grants
- fellowships
- awards
- competitions
- conferences and training

The core idea:

> Customers post an application mission. Vetted Scholastiar Forwarders accept, bid, or negotiate to execute the mission professionally.

This service gives users a human-powered alternative or backup to the AI Apply Agent, especially for complex, high-stakes, external, unclear, or sensitive applications.

The emotional promise:

> You do not have to apply alone, and you do not have to trust automation blindly.

The service must keep the greener-pasture mission at the center. Forwarders should help customers apply to opportunities that can realistically support work abroad, study abroad, funded travel, relocation, migration, international credibility, or movement toward the customer's country of choice.

## Apply For Me Board

Every opportunity card across the platform should be able to add eligible opportunities to an Apply For Me board when the user's subscription allows human application support.

The board should collect opportunities the user wants a vetted Scholastiar Forwarder to handle.

Board items should show:

- opportunity title and category
- country or region
- application deadline/window
- estimated application effort
- success/fit score
- documents required
- expected human effort
- budget or credit estimate
- review-before-submit preference
- follow-up preference
- current Forwarder/campaign status

If a user's subscription does not include Apply For Me, opportunity cards should show a locked or upgrade state for the board action while preserving save, view, and manual apply actions.

## Problem Being Solved

Many users want opportunities but do not have the time, confidence, technical ability, or application skill to apply properly at scale.

The service solves:

- repetitive application work
- external portal difficulty
- CAPTCHA/login blocks that AI cannot complete
- complex scholarship, grant, university, and fellowship applications
- poor application organization
- lack of follow-up
- missed deadlines
- weak document matching
- confusion about application requirements
- user fatigue
- low application volume
- lack of human judgment in sensitive applications
- need for proof that applications were actually submitted

Human Forwarders help when:

- external portals are difficult
- forms are poorly designed
- applications need human nuance
- documents need careful matching
- universities or organizations require email follow-up
- essays need manual review
- grant proposals need detailed packaging
- applications require multiple portal accounts
- the user wants premium concierge execution

## Core Workflows

### Customer Proposal Creation

Customers create structured application missions.

Example requests:

- apply to 50 visa-sponsored software engineering jobs in the UK
- apply to 20 fully funded scholarships for master's programs in Europe
- apply to 10 Russian medical universities under $5,000 tuition
- apply to 15 grants for a youth education nonprofit
- apply to 8 fellowships related to public policy and leadership
- apply to 12 conferences with travel funding
- submit a project to 10 startup competitions
- apply to 5 international awards for a social impact project

Customers can set:

- budget
- number of applications
- category
- target countries
- mobility goal
- preferred destination country or region
- deadline
- application quality level
- whether AI tools may be used
- whether human-written customization is required
- whether follow-up communication is included
- whether portal account creation is allowed
- whether payment/application fees are allowed
- whether review is required before submission

### Proposal Types

Proposal types:

- fixed number of applications
- daily or weekly application target
- category-specific campaign
- country-specific campaign
- deadline-based urgent campaign
- premium application package
- follow-up-only package
- research and apply package
- apply, track, and report package
- full concierge package

Example:

```txt
Apply to 30 visa-sponsored jobs in Canada within 14 days.
Budget: $120
Includes: CV tailoring, cover letters, proof of submission
Review before submit: No
Follow-up included: Yes
```

### Forwarder Marketplace

Forwarders can:

- accept the offer
- reject the offer
- bid a different price
- propose a different scope
- ask clarifying questions
- request missing documents
- negotiate timeline
- submit milestone plan

Example Forwarder response:

> I can apply to 30 jobs in 10 days for $150, including cover letter customization and proof of submission. I recommend excluding roles with long assessments unless paid separately.

### Forwarder Role

A Scholastiar Forwarder is a vetted human operator trained to execute applications professionally.

Forwarder responsibilities may include:

- reviewing customer profile
- checking eligibility
- shortlisting opportunities
- filling application forms
- tailoring answers
- uploading documents
- creating portal accounts where allowed
- submitting applications
- capturing proof of submission
- tracking statuses
- sending follow-up emails
- reporting progress
- flagging missing documents
- escalating sensitive questions
- maintaining confidentiality

### Forwarder Vetting

Forwarders should be screened, verified, trained, rated, and monitored.

Vetting can include:

- identity verification
- background/profile review
- language proficiency
- writing test
- application accuracy test
- data privacy training
- category specialization test
- platform workflow training
- ethics/compliance agreement
- probation period
- quality score monitoring

Forwarder specializations:

- job application forwarder
- scholarship forwarder

- grant forwarder
- fellowship forwarder
- awards/competitions forwarder
- conference/training forwarder

- language specialist

### Bidding And Negotiation

Forwarders should respond to proposals with:

- accept price
- counteroffer
- timeline adjustment

- required documents
- clarification questions
- milestone proposal

The system should support structured negotiation before work begins.

### Milestone System

Large projects should be milestone-based.

Example milestones:

- shortlist approved
- first 10 applications submitted
- next 20 applications submitted
- all applications submitted
- follow-up round completed
- final report delivered

Funds can be released by milestone to protect both customer and Forwarder.

### Escrow And Payments

Recommended payment model:

- customer funds milestone or project
- money held in escrow
- Forwarder completes work
- proof submitted
- customer approves or auto-release occurs after review window
- platform takes commission

Possible platform fees:

- commission from Forwarder earnings
- service fee from customer
- premium campaign fee
- urgent campaign fee
- verified specialist surcharge
- concierge management fee

### Application Proof System

Every submitted application should have proof:

- submission screenshot
- confirmation email
- confirmation number
- application URL
- date/time
- documents uploaded
- answers submitted
- portal account used
- status
- forwarder notes

Forwarders should not be able to claim work without proof.

### Customer Control And Safety

Customers should control sensitive actions.

They can decide:

- whether Forwarder can submit without review
- whether Forwarder can create portal accounts
- whether Forwarder can use AI-generated answers
- whether Forwarder can send emails
- whether Forwarder can upload sensitive documents
- whether Forwarder can pay application fees
- whether Forwarder must ask before answering sensitive questions

Sensitive actions requiring approval:

- payment
- legal declarations
- signatures
- visa/immigration declarations
- financial information
- recommender information
- references
- account creation
- high-stakes essay submission

### Confidentiality And Access Control

Forwarders may need access to sensitive documents. Access must be controlled.

Features:

- document permission controls
- expiring access links
- watermarking sensitive files
- download restrictions where possible
- audit logs of document views/downloads
- role-based document access
- masked sensitive data where possible
- NDA/ethics agreement for Forwarders
- customer can revoke access anytime

## Risks And Constraints

Key risks:

- Forwarders submitting incorrect information
- privacy breaches
- document misuse
- unauthorized actions
- missed deadlines
- fake proof
- low-quality applications
- disputes over scope
- escrow/payment complexity
- customer missing documents
- Forwarder impersonation or fraud
- legal declarations and signatures

Important constraints:

- Forwarders must never fabricate qualifications
- Forwarders must never forge documents
- Forwarders must not impersonate recommenders
- Forwarders must not submit false financial information
- Forwarders must not sign documents without approval
- Forwarders must not pay fees without approval
- Forwarders must not bypass security controls
- Forwarders must not apply outside agreed scope
- document access must be permissioned and auditable
- proof of submission is required for paid milestones

Core rule:

> Forwarders help customers apply professionally. They do not create fake applicants.
