# Awards

Status: Unified Platform Service

## Feature Vision

Scholastiar.ai Awards is an AI-powered award discovery and nomination engine that helps students, professionals, founders, researchers, creators, NGOs, and organizations find relevant awards, prepare compelling nomination packages, and manage award applications globally.

Awards are not only funding opportunities. They are credibility infrastructure. Winning or even being shortlisted for the right award can improve a user's CV, employer visibility, scholarship strength, fellowship strength, grant credibility, investor trust, and global reputation.

Core promise:

> The user explains their achievements once. Scholastiar.ai turns them into credible, award-specific nomination packages.

The system should help users discover awards, check eligibility, map their real achievements to award criteria, generate truthful answers, prepare supporting evidence, request nominations or references, submit applications, and track outcomes.

Every award opportunity should also be understood as a global mobility asset. The system should help users prioritize awards that can improve international credibility, strengthen visa, scholarship, fellowship, grant, university, or job applications, provide travel or ceremony access, and make the user more competitive when moving toward another country or global market.

## Problem Being Solved

Award applications are difficult because users often:

- do not know which awards exist
- underestimate their eligibility
- struggle to explain achievements powerfully
- repeat the same biography and impact story across forms
- fail to collect evidence before deadlines
- do not understand judging criteria
- miss nomination requirements
- write generic award statements
- cannot translate work into measurable impact
- struggle with reference or recommender requests
- lose track of deadlines and award stages
- cannot easily reuse award wins across CVs, profiles, grants, fellowships, and scholarships

Many qualified people do not apply because awards feel intimidating or hidden.

## Core Workflows

### Award Discovery

Users should browse awards by:

- country or region
- international recognition or target-country credibility
- field or industry
- career stage
- student/professional/founder/researcher category
- age eligibility
- nationality eligibility
- award type
- prize value
- travel, relocation, or global exposure benefits
- funding or non-funding recognition
- nomination required or self-apply
- evidence requirements
- judging criteria
- deadline
- verification status
- prestige level
- application difficulty

### Award Profile Pages

Each award should include:

- award overview
- organizer/provider
- eligible countries
- eligible applicant types
- categories
- prize or recognition benefits
- nomination/application method
- required documents
- required evidence
- judging criteria
- past winners where available
- deadline
- official application link
- verified status
- scam/risk notes
- estimated preparation time
- AI fit summary

### AI Award Matching

The AI should match users to awards based on:

- achievements
- leadership history
- academic record
- target-country credibility value
- professional experience
- founder/startup progress
- research outputs
- community impact
- creative portfolio
- nationality/location
- age/career stage
- award category
- evidence readiness

Recommendations can be grouped as:

- strong fit
- possible fit
- ambitious
- missing evidence
- not eligible

### Achievement Profile Builder

Awards need a reusable achievement profile.

The builder should capture:

- achievement title
- role of the user
- timeline
- problem/context
- actions taken
- measurable results
- beneficiaries or audience
- media/proof links
- certificates
- references
- testimonials
- awards already won
- impact metrics

The same profile can feed CVs, scholarships, fellowships, grants, employer profiles, and PersonalityAI CV.

### One-To-One Award Question Mapping

Award questions should be mapped to AI-generated answers.

Common questions:

- Describe your achievement.
- Why do you deserve this award?
- What impact has your work created?
- What challenges did you overcome?
- What makes your work innovative?
- How have you demonstrated leadership?
- How will this recognition help you grow?
- What evidence supports your nomination?

Each answer should show:

- original question
- AI draft
- achievement facts used
- evidence used
- missing evidence warning
- edit field
- approval checkbox

### Nomination And Reference Support

Many awards require someone else to nominate the applicant.

The platform should help users:

- identify suitable nominators
- generate nomination request emails
- prepare a nominator briefing document
- track nomination status
- remind nominators
- collect reference letters
- attach evidence packages

### Evidence Readiness System

The system should track evidence such as:

- certificates
- media mentions
- project links
- publications
- portfolio links
- testimonials
- recommendation letters
- metrics screenshots
- photos/videos
- proof of leadership
- proof of impact

It should warn when an award requires evidence the user has not uploaded.

### Application Tracker

Suggested statuses:

- discovered
- saved
- eligibility checked
- evidence missing
- nomination requested
- answers generated
- ready for review
- submitted
- shortlisted
- interview/judging stage
- awarded
- not selected
- certificate/recognition received

### Post-Award Reputation Layer

After a user wins or is shortlisted, the platform should help them:

- add the award to their AI CV
- update PersonalityAI CV
- generate LinkedIn announcement text
- generate employer-facing credibility summary
- attach the award to scholarship/fellowship/grant applications
- store certificates and proof
- create a verified achievement badge later

## AI Opportunities

### Award Narrative Engine

The AI should turn real achievements into compelling award language by improving:

- clarity
- impact framing
- leadership framing
- innovation framing
- evidence alignment
- judging criteria alignment
- concision and tone

### Impact Evidence Engine

The AI should help users identify measurable impact:

- people reached
- money raised
- revenue generated
- research output
- community outcomes
- media reach
- product adoption
- academic results
- organizational change

### Legal And Truthful Award Generation

The AI must never fabricate:

- awards
- achievements
- metrics
- beneficiaries
- leadership roles
- media mentions
- certificates
- references
- testimonials
- publications

The AI may improve how real achievements are explained.

Core rule:

> Scholastiar.ai helps users present real achievements powerfully. It does not invent recognition.

## Risks And Constraints

Key risks:

- fake awards
- exaggerated achievements
- fabricated metrics
- outdated deadlines
- unclear nomination rules
- AI sounding too boastful
- privacy issues around evidence

Important constraints:

- AI must never invent achievements
- evidence should be clearly sourced
- user approval is required before submission
- verification status must be visible
